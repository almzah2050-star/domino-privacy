Privacy Policy – Domino Game

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Domino ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our mobile game.

By using Domino, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

Account Information

When you register or sign in, we may collect:

  • Email address.
  • Username or display name.
  • Account authentication information.
  • Unique player account identifier.

Device Information

  • Device model and operating system.
  • Device identifiers.
  • IP address.
  • Language and regional settings.
  • Technical diagnostics and crash reports.

Gameplay Information

  • Game scores and match history.
  • Player preferences and settings.
  • Selected domino tile and table designs.
  • Multiplayer room information.
  • Online gameplay activity.

Advertising Information

Our advertising partners may collect:

  • Advertising identifiers.
  • IP address.
  • Device information.
  • Advertising impressions and interactions.
  • Information used for personalized advertising, where permitted.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage player accounts.
  • Authenticate players.
  • Provide online multiplayer functionality.
  • Create and manage private and public rooms.
  • Synchronize multiplayer matches.
  • Save player preferences and purchased or unlocked game designs.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Improve game performance and user experience.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.

3. Advertising Services

Domino uses third-party advertising services, including Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads.

These services may collect device information, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, and advertising interaction information to deliver advertisements, measure performance, prevent fraud, and provide personalized or non-personalized advertising.

You can manage advertising preferences through your device settings and available consent options.

Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

4. Multiplayer Services

Domino uses Unity Gaming Services, including Unity Authentication, Unity Lobby, and Unity Relay, to provide online multiplayer functionality.

These services may process:

  • Player account identifiers.
  • Usernames and player profiles.
  • Room IDs and room settings.
  • Connection and network information.
  • Matchmaking and session information.
  • Gameplay synchronization data.

This information is used to create, join, and manage multiplayer games.

Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We may share necessary information with trusted third-party providers that help us operate the game, including:

  • Unity Technologies – Authentication, Lobby, Relay, and advertising services.
  • Unity LevelPlay – Advertising mediation.
  • Other technical service providers required to operate and maintain the game.

These providers process information according to their respective privacy policies and applicable laws.

We do not sell personal information.

6. Data Retention and Security

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, maintain player accounts, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Account and Data Deletion

Players may request deletion of their accounts and associated personal information.

To request account deletion, contact us using the email address provided below.

Upon receiving a valid request, we will delete the relevant account information unless retention is required by applicable law or legitimate security obligations.

Domino is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.
